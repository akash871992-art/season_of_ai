AI history timeline · 1943 to today

Seasons of AI

Artificial intelligence did not rise in a straight line. It moved through summers of hype and funding and winters when money and belief dried up. Then, after 2012, GPUs, big data and cloud computing finally caught up with ideas that were decades old. Tap a band to jump to that era.

Two ideas that frame the story

The IEEE overview splits AI history into ages. These two distinctions explain most of the swings above.

SYMBOLIC → CONNECTIONIST

Rules vs. learning

Early AI (1956–1990s) hand-coded logic and rules: expert systems, Deep Blue. Modern AI learns patterns from data with neural networks. The shift happened when data and compute became cheap enough for learning to beat hand-written rules.

NARROW AI vs. AGI

Specialist vs. generalist

Every system here is narrow AI: excellent at defined tasks like chess, image labels or text. Artificial General Intelligence would match humans across any intellectual task. It remains a goal and a debate, not an achievement.

Leading players today

The current race is fought on three fronts: models, chips and cloud.

MODELS

OpenAI

GPT series and ChatGPT, which brought generative AI to the public in 2022. Backed by Microsoft.

MODELS + SCIENCE

Google DeepMind

AlphaGo, AlphaFold and the Gemini models. Google researchers also invented the Transformer.

MODELS

Anthropic

Claude models, with a focus on AI safety research. Founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers.

OPEN WEIGHTS

Meta

Llama models released openly, so anyone can download and build on them.

CHIPS

NVIDIA

GPUs and CUDA software power most AI training. This made it one of the world's most valuable companies.

CLOUD

Microsoft, Amazon, Google

Azure, AWS and Google Cloud rent out the massive compute that model training needs.

OPEN WEIGHTS

DeepSeek

Chinese lab whose low-cost R1 reasoning model in 2025 showed frontier AI was not limited to US giants.

TREND

Compute curve

Training compute for leading models has doubled roughly every six months since 2010, versus about every 20 months before (Our World in Data).
